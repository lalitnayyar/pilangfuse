## What TraceBrief AI does

A user enters one topic.

Then three agents execute the workflow:

- **Web Research Agent** finds relevant web sources
- **Video Research Agent** finds relevant YouTube references
- **Report Writer Agent** produces a shareable markdown brief

On top of that, the product includes:
- **Langfuse tracing** for visibility
- **live activity UI** for confidence and usability
- **retry + fallback logic** for resilience
- **browser UI + API** for flexible adoption
- **in-app settings** for easier operations
- **Docker-ready deployment** for fast rollout

That is what turns AI capability into a sellable product.
